Everything sits at its real position and real size: the planets where they are today, 1,982 naked-eye stars at their measured distances, the Milky Way, its neighbours and the cosmic web out to the edge of the observable universe. Brightness is compressed so faint things stay visible, stars twinkle gently for life, and fast processes (orbits, explosions, mergers) are sped up. The few objects too small to see next to their surroundings (a quasar's engine, a kilonova) are drawn magnified, and the ruler shows their true size. The music is generated in your browser as you listen. The Halo, the crescent flagship that wanders between wonders, is the one invented thing here.
